⚽ Career Path

Guess the footballer from the clubs they played for. Each wrong guess shows the next club. The sooner you get it, the more points.

How to play

  • You start with the player's nation and position.
  • Their clubs then appear in order, one at a time, each time you guess wrong or ask for the next clue.
  • The last clue is their initials.
  • A correct guess on the first clue scores 6 points, then 5, 4, and so on, down to 1.
